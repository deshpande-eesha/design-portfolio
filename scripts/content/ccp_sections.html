What is the Care Companion Program's process ?

  1. Identification & Prioritization — Family members are identified as caregivers, focusing on public hospitals to support marginalized populations
  2. Healthcare Staff Training — Nurses are trained to teach caregivers caregiving skills using structured methods
  3. Family Caregiver Training — Caregivers receive on-site training with videos and flipcharts. Nurses track sessions using a mobile app
  4. Ongoing Support — Caregivers get reference materials and post-discharge support via WhatsApp enhancing communication

How were Nurses tracking the sessions before ?

Nurses in government hospitals faced challenges with paper based tracking - missing records, offline issues and time-consuming data entry. A simple mobile app was introduced to ease tracking with a user-friendly, minimal design for non-tech-savvy users

Previously using - Pen and paper
Using pen and paper is slow, error-prone, and hard to organize. Records can be lost, photos can't be stored, and manual data entry takes time. It also lacks real-time updates and isn't eco-friendly.

Improved experience with Mobile App
Using a mobile app makes session tracking faster and more reliable. It stores photos, works offline, updates in real time, and reduces manual effort.

My role

Designing the Care Companion Program (HEP) for nurses, supervisors, implementation, and monitoring teams - enabling government hospital staff across India, Nepal, Bangladesh, and Indonesia to:

  • Mark training sessions conducted for caregivers and upload supporting photos
  • Access a learning section with educational materials for reference
  • Review past sessions to track their progress
  • Manage profiles with essential details
  • Multi-language interface to support diverse linguistic backgrounds
  • Role-Based Access Control for a seamless, nurse-focused experience

Design Process

  • Conducted user interviews with nurses to understand their challenges in session entry
  • Refined the UI based on testing insights and delivered the final design output
  • Attended CCP sessions in Bangalore's Government hospitals to observe real-world usage
  • Developed a comprehensive end-to-end design system for consistency
  • Created wireframe iterations and developed an interactive prototype for nurses to test
  • Designed the RBAC mobile app for Supervisors, Nurses and Implementation Team Members
  • Designed usability testing to gather actionable feedback
  • Implemented a multi-language interface to support various regional languages
