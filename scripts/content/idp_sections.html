What is Individual Development Plan ?

An Individual Development Plan (IDP) is a personalized roadmap that helps employees set goals and identify steps to enhance their skills and achieve career objectives

What was the Design process ?

  • Managers struggled to track progress and run effective 1:1s
  • HR needed visibility without micromanaging

Research - Challenges employees face

Employees lacked a clear, structured way to set development goals, track progress, and align with organizational expectations - leading to confusion, missed opportunities, and limited manager support.

Qualitative

1) Stakeholder Interviews Spoke with employees, managers and HR teams across different departments to understand current challenges around development planning.

2) Contextual Inquiry Observed how people currently tracked goals - Notion or Google Docs for privacy, indicating a lack of trust or comfort in the platform.

Helped uncover the need for a "draft" mode and visibility controls.

3) Client Conversations (via PM & Sales Team) Joined product walkthroughs and feedback sessions with clients to hear pain points firsthand and understand expectations from an IDP tool

Quantitative

1) Support ticket Review Analyzed tickets related to performance on goal tracking module.

  • Confusion about goal ownership and visibility
  • Friction in initiating goal-setting

2) Feature usage patterns (Baseline)

Collected feedback from internal users who attempted development planning using competitor tools. They highlighted the need for sample goals, flexible flows and clarity on who can see what

Research Conclusion

Ambiguous Struggle in their careers due to unclear goals and a lack of direction for growth

Disconnected They often miss growth opportunities and feel disconnected from their organization's goals

Fear and challenges Without a clear plan, they risk falling behind and missing key growth opportunities

Organizational alignment Need for a solution that helps individuals set goals, build skills and align with organizational objectives

Why is an IDP important ?

An Individual Development Plan (IDP) is essential as it provides a clear roadmap for personal and professional growth, helping individuals reach their full potential and achieve career goals

Goal setting An IDP helps individuals define goals for professional growth

Self Reflection It encourages reflection on strengths, weaknesses and improvement areas

Skill Development An IDP outlines clear steps to build new skills or strengthen existing ones

Career advancement By targeting growth areas and setting goals, an IDP supports career advancement

Aligning with organization IDPs align personal goals with organizational objectives, boosting productivity and impact

Continuous improvement IDP provides a structured path for continuous learning in a changing environment

Individual Development Plan creation process

Select Role Choose your current, future, or general role to anchor your development goals

Select Competency Pick the skill or competency area you'd like to focus on improving or growing into

Enter Goal Define a clear, personalized development goal aligned with your selected competency

Select Timeline Set a target completion date to track progress and maintain accountability

Add what Help from Organization Specify the support or resources you need from the company to achieve your goal

Add action items for Goals to achieve Break down your goal into smaller, concrete steps or milestones

Notify Manager Share your plan with your manager to align expectations and begin collaboration

Check in progress of Goal Update progress regularly to reflect achievements and flag blockers

End Growth Plan Mark the plan as complete once goals and actions are fulfilled

Last Check-In Wrap up with a final reflection or feedback entry to close the loop on the development cycle

Design Development

Constraints

Adding a draft stage before Submitting to Manager
Problem: Employees were hesitant to share incomplete IDPs, leading to delays and workarounds like using Notion or Google Docs for privacy.

Solution:

  • Added a "Draft" status to keep plans private until shared
  • Enabled autosave and "last edited" timestamps to support gradual progress
  • Created a safe space for employees to explore goals before sharing

Adding Visibility Controls for the IDP

Problem: Unclear visibility of IDPs caused employee hesitation. Some asked HR about who could see their plans, while HR and managers needed access with clear boundaries.

Solution:

  • Introduced role-based visibility settings: Default: shared with direct manager and optional: share with skip-level or HRBP
  • Allowed marking goals as "private"
  • Added visibility icons with tooltips for transparency

"What help do you need from the organization?"

Problem: Employees listed goals but rarely requested support. Managers didn't know how to assist, and HR couldn't spot broader resource gaps.

Solution:

  • Added an optional "What support do you need?" section - Included structured choices (mentorship, training, etc.) + free-text field
  • Built an HR dashboard to show aggregated support needs across teams

Ideations

Kanban style

Pros of Kanban-style IDP:

  • Easy to see progress
  • Flexible and easy to update

Cons:

  • Can be too simple for big-picture planning
  • Might get messy if not organized
  • Focuses more on tasks than strategy
idp-ideation-kanban.jpg

Form style

Pros of Form-style IDP:

  • Structured and organized
  • Good for long-term planning
  • Step by step process

Cons:

  • Can feel rigid or formal
  • Not great for tracking progress
  • Less engaging than visual formats
idp-ideation-form.jpg

Hybrid timeline view with editable goal cards

Pros:

  • You can see when goals and actions happen
  • Easy to edit goals as priorities shift
  • Combines structure (timeline) with adaptability (cards)

Cons

  • Gets cluttered with too many cards
  • Requires maintenance to stay updated

Feedback and Testing

  • Shadowing client HR demos
  • Internal launch at our company
  • UAT testing

Shadowing client HR demos — Observed client HR reps demo the product to employees, gathering feedback on reactions

Internal launch at our company — Let our own employees use the product first to gather feedback before wider release

UAT testing — Created 28 use cases to cover key user flows and edge cases, validated during UAT

Metrics to understand usage

  • % of Employees with an Active IDP — Indicates adoption across the organization. Can be segmented by team, department, or level
  • Manager Feedback Rate per IDP — How often managers are commenting or contributing to employee growth plans
  • % of IDPs Submitted to Managers — Measures how many plans progress beyond the draft stage into collaborative use
  • % of IDPs with "Support from Organization" Section Completed — Gauges how often employees are expressing resource or learning needs
  • Average Time to Complete an IDP — Helps identify friction in the creation process
  • Most Selected Competencies — Useful for HR and L&D to spot trends or gaps in organizational skill focus
  • % of IDPs with at Least One Goal Tracked — Shows whether plans are meaningful or just placeholders
  • Completion Rate of Goals Within Timeline — Tracks effectiveness and feasibility of plans, and employee follow-through
  • % of Goals with Progress Check-ins Logged — Reflects active engagement and follow-through on development actions
  • IDP Influence on Performance Review Scores or Engagement — Correlating IDP completion with improved quarterly review outcomes or higher employee satisfaction can make a strong business case

Key B2B Learnings

Complex job flows

B2B HR users have multi-step workflows. For the IDP feature:

  • Designed for asynchronous collaboration between employees, managers & HR
  • Prioritized flexible flows to adapt to various org structures and review cycles

No direct access to the users

Feedback from all users wasn't always possible

  • Relied on proxies like HRBPs, customer success managers, and support tickets
  • Designed with empathy and made educated assumptions, to be validated over time
  • Incorporated privacy and psychological safety through draft modes and granular visibility settings

Long feedback cycles

B2B tools linked to quarterly reviews need time for meaningful feedback. Full impact was clear only after performance cycles

  • Tracked early signals like draft engagement, manager interactions, and initial feedback

Productivity over Aesthetics

B2B users prioritize clarity, efficiency and trust

  • Focused on speed, sensible defaults, and simplicity
  • Minimized mental load with fewer clicks, shortcuts, and clear status indicators
