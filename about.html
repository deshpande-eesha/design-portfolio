About Me

a human-centered designer, passionate about impactful design 🌱

I turn complex business workflows into scalable, human-centered digital experiences. With 7+ years across B2B SaaS, public health tech, and enterprise HR platforms, I specialize in design systems, WCAG accessibility, and end-to-end product strategy.

Currently at Procore, I lead UX for capital planning tools used by construction owners managing multi-billion dollar portfolios. Previously at Noora Health and xto10x, I've shipped mobile health products at scale and AI-assisted HR platforms. I hold an M.Des from IIT Delhi and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Pune University.

Here are some of my writings that capture various facets of my journey!

Moving on is easy. It is what you are leaving behind matters. We all are going to leave behind a city for us to live in and also the future generations to come.

Imagine a city of the future. No one is sure or aware of what the future holds for us. But do we visualize a clean surrounding, cars flying while robots doing the work? If we look at the present condition of the world, the cities are overpopulated, crowded and polluted. The reason for all of this may be because of migration. Many other people are attracted by the opportunities available in cities. This can be the reason why more than half of the population live in cities. Cities have the centres and collections of enterprise and innovation. Researches predict that around 75% of the people will be living in the cities by 2050. But exactly the cities will be by that time? The time is ripe as the clock is ticking. I believe it is time for us to design our future smart societies. But, we have to keep in mind that the future cities should attain the balance and satisfy the needs of every growing population and the current people who have been living in cities for years and decades.

It is a fact that the present and the past cities have been shaped by people according to their timely needs, then the cities of the future must be shaped by ideas. One of the ideas is that smarter equals greener. Sustainable cities would be carbon-neutral cities. With the electric vehicles coming up and people valuing the car-pooling services, I believe that the quality of the environment will improve. We will be able to open our windows and breathe in natural air. Visions of the green city may include skyscrapers where the residential and commercial spaces can have green roofs and vegetable gardens. I have a strong pressing need for such greenification. I dream of the world in green colour!

Emotion centric data and artificial intelligence may have the answer for this. How if we put all the data about a city's performance at one place where others can also have a view; suggest, learn or adapt a thoughtful activity. This will also help cities and nations and ultimately mankind to work in efficient collaboration. Internet of things could help us get into sustainable developments with some unbelievable assistance. Human-centred and with technical help can create smart, green and livable cities.

The power must lie with the citizens. The designs and ideas of the cities will revolve around the people. There might be some communication gap between what people want and truly need and what companies what to sell, but it will eventually all come to a sweet balance. Art will come into the picture. People will start responding and there will be social, cultural, and behavioral progress. A happy society will start emerging

The city and urban landscape planning or reshaping will have a powerful effect on how we live and let live people in the future of the world. Cities will have something for everyone when made with help everybody keeping in mind inclusivity.

In the city of the future, moral education will get its due attention and provide children with the latest knowledge and techniques in the field of their interest. Everyone will live in harmony and peace; different religion, caste, cultures will learn and enrich themselves with help of each other by respecting the differences. The cultural centres will be open to all to share ideas, meet different people and learn about a specific culture. Artistic things will be given prominent importance, museums will be enriched. Theatre, dance, music and literature will be embedded into the city for further expression. In my vision of the city of tomorrow, people will live and work in harmony. Rationality, kindness and humanity will be celebrated. Hate and prejudice will remain things taught in history. The city will be clean, safe, efficient, multicultural, and built on an exchange of ideas. Imagine all the people living life in peace. The future cities will have people's decisions and the government's participation rather than the government's decision and people's participation. I remember John Lenon's words - "You may say I'm a dreamer, but I'm not the only one. I hope someday you'll join me, and the world will be like one"

I am aware that future cities cannot solve the current challenges being faced. But the cities should not ignore the current situation and start from zero. I am not dreaming of a city that is an ideal or perfect scenario that is free of problems. I believe future cities are keeping in mind the mistakes done and learning from them and definitely not repeating them. The aim or agenda of the future cities can be solving the current urban social problems. This can lead to rational development. Designers, architects, city planners can be practically advised on what all needs to be taken into consideration for the dream futuristic logical smart urban living.

I wish that I'm heading towards a place of great positive enthusiasm, where I can work collaboratively, meet new friends, enjoy various cultural activities and get mesmerized looking at man-made wonders. If a city could be designed for the enjoyment of life, rather than for the unwelcomed necessary activities involved in earning a living, what would that city life be like?

As cities go, so goes the planet!

Virtue is having qualities of good morals. They are almost recognized in all cultures as the basic qualities of well being. Good values can define the moral excellence of someone. And virtue is this moral excellence of a person. Morally intelligent people have a character that is valued as good

Vulnerability according to me is being open to feel and express feelings and thoughts of happiness, fear, sadness, pain, and hopes with others with or without the fear of being rejected or judged. Being vulnerable helps you accept someone openly, wholeheartedly. I believe we become more connected to ourselves when we are vulnerable by being genuine and authentic. We try to understand the other person with kindness and compassion. Vulnerability is the ability to trust someone. I think the best kind of human relationships are built on trust, genuineness, acceptance and vulnerability. I believe vulnerability is a strength rather than a weakness.

Not being vulnerable is not being the true you! Hiding your true self is selfish and will not create strong, genuine bonds. I believe not being vulnerable can also lead to loneliness as it creates obstacles in communications. Not being vulnerable means you are not willing to take the risk of being seen as you truly are.

All connections & relationships require connection. Deeper human interactions and connections happen when someone takes the risk of being vulnerable. Nobody knows what might happen. But it takes courage and honesty to in being vulnerable

Perfectionism is another threat to being vulnerable. Perfectionism is a fear of getting anything and everything possible right. It doesn't let you meet your true self. It is an obsession of needing the control of everything which is toxic for the person suffering from perfectionism and their connection

Being vulnerable is willing to feel the uncomfortable emotions rather than avoiding them. We need to feel the feeling while it is being felt. And here I present empathy! Vulnerability is very closely connected to empathy. Without vulnerability we can't connect with our own experiences.

Empathy for me is the ability to feel others, to imagine oneself in another's shoes (position) and trying to feel and think like that person. Empathy gives you a compassionate perspective towards society. Intelligence, intellectualism without empathy, kindness or a sense of service has no depth. Being empathetic towards oneself helps us emotionally understand ourselves. It is our natural need to be felt understood. And feeling understood enhances our connection, help and support for each other. And it is difficult to connect to others if we cannot address our own emotions. And so I think that being empathetic is vital for interconnectivity by being emotionally available for us.

Genuine connections are made with empathy while sympathy is something that can create separation or disconnection. Empathy helps us resonate, understand and trust the other person. I believe that with sympathy, there is more of a solving rather than understanding the situation and connection

Change is inevitable. It is constant. We are not going to be the same what we are today after 10 years. Reluctant to change is being afraid of vulnerability. We need to adapt to the evolving experiences. This dealing with new experiences helps us grow as a person. This growth will be much constructive and healthier if we are ready to face the process by being vulnerable rather than running away from it. Empathy and vulnerability will help towards a healthier and much acceptance oriented self-growth.

"Empathy is a choice and it's a vulnerable choice. Because in order to connect with you, I have to connect with something in myself that knows that feeling" says Brené Brown

I think the one thing that is unique and equally amazing is our brain. I believe the humanness of a human is in the brain and the mind. The brain is the visible and tangible part of the body where innumerable neurons contribute to our mind. Mind is the intangible part full of feelings, thoughts, emotions, thoughts and consciousness

Mind is a tricky concept. I think I know what a mind is because I have one - or because I am one. You might feel the same. But the minds of others are not directly observable. We assume that others have minds somewhat like ours - filled with beliefs and desires - but we can only infer those mental states. We cannot see, feel, or touch them. We largely rely on language to inform each other about what is on our minds
- Thomas Suddendorf

Our ability to communicate through languages or the moralities we have and reasoning ability are something that distinguishes us. We have developed our own culture and there is diversity in these cultures - be it in terms of religion, castes, locations, gender etc. Humans are also social animals. We are interdependent and also develop our relationships with other humans. Humans also can communicate and show their emotions by using empathy

We live in the present but also make and imagine our lives by planning our future and by working towards it. We are aware and live in the duality of the known and unknown. Humans are aware that someday every individual is going to die. We know that we have a specific time to live and love.

Now with the increasing artificial intelligence, robots and machines, we know that these technologies can fight us with our thinking capabilities. But what differentiates us from these fast-growing technologies? I believe that the basic difference between these technologies and humans is that we have feelings and emotions. These unique abilities of ours make us empathize, be compassionate and also dream towards a moral good. We can build deep connections of trust, care and love.

This wonderful ability to emote plays a vital role in how we behave and think. They influence us to make decisions; big or small and we act accordingly. All of this based on cognitive bias. Sometimes we do think that we are taking decisions solely based on logic or rationality, but emotions play a key role

Our emotional intelligence helps us manage and understand these emotions which play a vital role in our decision making.

I care, therefore I am; I think, therefore I am; I hope, therefore I am; I imagine, therefore I am. I am ethical, therefore I am. I have a purpose, therefore I am. I pause and reflect, therefore I am.
- Seidman

Our evolution and genes define and lead what we are and our surroundings define our behaviour, which aligns with our cognition, values, and beliefs.

The only way for me to be human is for you to reflect my humanity at me. There is no way for us to be human without other humans
- Philosophy of Ubuntu

Awareness, acceptance and action are often defined as the three A's of change. A real change. A change towards betterment. I think making a complete 180 change in one go can be scary which may nudge our procrastination. Thus making us stay away from the change. A step-by-step process can be can help us build the impulse for mind and body change.

Ask yourself these questions?

  • What kind of world would I create if I had the opportunity to do so?
  • Would I rather live in an environment filled with chaos, anxiety, danger, or serenity, peace, and hope?
  • How about giving change a genuine try?

What is awareness?

Awareness is a conscious state of being. It is being open and curious to know, understand, perceive and reflect upon some situation or event. But in this context, I think it is like being aware of how something will have behavioural changes in one's life. Awareness is being conscious. Asking questions like - 'What is it about me that I am not able to see?' or 'What is it that is making me react like this?' or 'What is it about me that is making me behave like this?' Awareness is the psychological step. Awareness is the booster for the process of real growth

What is acceptance?

Acceptance is being aware that your behaviour is not ideal and you're accepting it without resisting that it's happening.

One has to be openly curious to accept all parts of themselves including the ones they do not like. It is hard accepting things when you are accustomed to your behaviour. It will be hard especially if you have the habit to control situations and people. Acceptance can be like a spiritual step. Dr David Daniels describes acceptance as "Opening the heart in kindness to self and others, especially to one's reactivity and upset." It's important, he says, to realize that "This doesn't mean capitulating, condoning, or agreeing with our own or others' behaviour."

What is the action?

When we have licensed the self-awareness and acceptance, comes the action part. When we are aware and have accepted what needs to be changed, then comes the real part. Real change will happen when you have accepted the reality rather than running from it. And constructive action will happen when we are aware of the reality, the truth. The real behavioural change will happen when we are aware and have accepted our thoughts and feelings. Personal growth will happen when we are curious and open to seeking something more. And it is of great strength if we decide to take some help for this. Practising acceptance and bringing it into action is the true sense of the word.

Moving through these steps gradually will give us the experience to create a real constructive positive change. Having an open mind and be curiousity will open many options. Believe me, the benefits or self growth are worth the wait.

Is it possible to manage the balance? Is there a need for balance? How can logic, rationality has creativity and emotion aligned to it? We all know that emotions play a vital role in our decision making even though we think our decision is solely based on logic. I believe our decisions are based on our cognitive bias - on what and how we think

It is said that our logic and rationality comes from the left side while our emotions or creativity comes from the right. While the idea of right or left-brain thinkers has been debunked, its popularity persists. Research also says that both hemispheres work together

Emotions are amazing because it makes us feel alive - be it sad, scared, happy or joyful. Emotions involve physical and cognitive changes which may or may not be understood by others. When an emotion is felt continuously for some time then it is called a mood. Rationality is based on logic. But I feel that when logic is judged on validity, then it is based on reason and our individual's knowledge

When decisions are solely based on thought and reason or logic, then it is being rational. And I think this is based on one's cognitive bias - one's thought process or the ability to agree to a reason. Then, rationality is like being able to believe something and then taking actions or decision based on the beliefs.

I wonder if our cognitive bias stops us from having a realistic view of the world as it is. We maybe just interact with our worlds that could result in maximum benefit for us. And our instincts or intuitions attract us like a magnet to trust them that our cognitive bias maybe becomes judgemental

How can we be rational and emotional as well? I think we should be logical and rational but still stay aligned with our emotions. Empathy is one thing that will help us relate and connect deeply with people. I also think that our reason and logical thinking will help us conclude but our motivation to take it into action is maybe led by emotions.

Thus for now, I strongly believe that thinking and emotion should have a good balance with each other. One thing without the other can turn out to be risky
