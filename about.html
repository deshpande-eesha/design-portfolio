About Me

Senior Product Designer specializing in enterprise B2B products, AI integration, and design systems. Driven by human-centered innovation and measurable impact

I turn complex business workflows into scalable, human-centered digital experiences. With 7+ years across B2B SaaS, public health tech, and enterprise HR platforms, I specialize in design systems, WCAG accessibility, and end-to-end product strategy.

Currently at Procore, I lead UX for capital planning used by construction owners on multi-billion-dollar portfolios. I am designing the AI on that product: a chatbot, executive summaries, and risk signals that stay inside the projects a person can open. Before this, at Noora Health and xto10x, I shipped a multilingual health app at scale and an HR platform used by high-growth companies. I hold an M.Des from IIT Delhi and a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Pune University.
