Fly you Girl

an educational booklet for girls on puberty: addressing body changes, period myths, sanitary products and PCOS

Research methodology

This is what I did for both primary and secondary research. It helped me understand the user and decide what to include in the booklet

Puberty Booklet Analysis

  • Conducted a thorough review of Menstrupedia, focusing on the structure, content, and approach of the booklet in association with Stayfree
  • Analyzed its effectiveness in educating girls about puberty, menstrual hygiene, and related subjects

Field Analysis and Interviews

  • Engaged in interviews and surveys among the target audience to assess their familiarity and perception of Menstrupedia
  • Explored the impact and usefulness of Menstrupedia in addressing puberty-related concerns and myths among girls

Exploration of Various Online Sources:

  • Analyzed social media platforms for discussions, posts, and community engagement regarding puberty education
  • Researched blog posts and articles addressing puberty education, menstrual hygiene, and related topics
  • Studied video content on YouTube to understand the visual approach in educating and engaging the target audience on puberty-related topics

Key Findings

Following both online and offline research, these observations came to light

Content Analysis of Menstrupedia

Identified strengths and weaknesses in the content, evaluating its ability to dispel myths and educate girls about puberty effectively

Perception of Whisper's Involvement

Explored how Whisper's association influenced the credibility and trustworthiness of the information presented in Menstrupedia.

Insights from Online Sources

Gathered prevalent misconceptions, concerns, and effective communication strategies from social media, blogs, articles, and videos to better address and educate the target audience.

User-Centric Design Features:

Prior to commencing the posts, I chose to focus on the following guidelines that I will bear in mind

Storybook Format - Personal Communication

The chosen storybook style will help create a personal and friendly conversation in the book, making it feel like it's talking directly to you

Colourful Design - Eye Catching Visuals

The book will include bright and fun pictures that will explain body changes and periods in an easy and clear way, making it more engaging

Language - Easy Words for Understanding

Simple words will be used to make it easy for kids aged 8-14 to read and understand. It will feel more like talking to a friend rather than reading a difficult book

Target Audience Focus - for Girls Growing Up

Even though parents might buy it, the book is specifically made for girls like you who will be starting to have periods and growing up

Educational Objective - Understanding Changes

The main goal will be to help understand the changes happening during puberty, ensuring it's clear and okay for you

Wireframing

By focusing on these design features after learning about what's needed and careful research, the book will be created to be easy to understand, engaging, and just right for girls aged 8-14 going through puberty.

Unconventional Design and Distinctive Layout

A square-shaped notebook's distinct design and layout offer an unconventional and visually appealing alternative to traditional rectangular or rounded notebooks, drawing attention due to its less common appearance

Ease of Handling

The square shape can provide a balanced feel and ease of handling, both in portrait and landscape orientations, offering comfort and versatility in use

Aesthetic Appeal

The clean lines and symmetrical design of a square notebook can contribute to its aesthetic appeal, creating a modern and visually pleasing object

Technical hurdles

Being my initial attempt at printing a 72-page booklet I did face some challenges but with help of others found a way through those

Printing Challenges and Color Alignment

Printing revealed discrepancies between digital and printed colors upon understanding CMYK. Adjustments were necessary to align colors accurately. After a trial print, I reworked the colors to ensure consistency between digital and printed outputs

Minimizing Paper Wastage with Square Shape

The square shape presented challenges in minimizing paper wastage during printing, leaving unused space. To optimize the remaining space, bookmarks were printed alongside the booklets, serving as an eco-friendly solution to reduce paper wastage
