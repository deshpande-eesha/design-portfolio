Fintech onboarding

Reducing drop-off in a multi-step KYC flow for a European neobank serving SME customers.

  • Role - Lead product designer
  • Team - Product, engineering, compliance
  • Outcome - 22% increase in completed applications
project-01-hero.jpg

Overview

The neobank's onboarding flow had high abandonment after identity verification. Users were unclear about document requirements and progress through the flow felt opaque under regulatory constraints.

Approach

I mapped the existing journey, ran moderated tests with 12 SME owners, and prototyped a step-by-step flow with upfront document guidance and persistent progress indicators aligned to PSD2 expectations.

Outcome

Completion rates rose 22% within six weeks of launch. Support tickets related to document upload dropped by 35%. The patterns were adopted into the bank's design system.
