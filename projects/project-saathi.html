Saathi

A scribe that reads the paper. You think. You answer.

Overview

Saathi is a digital AI exam scribe for visually impaired students. It reads competitive exam papers in English and Hindi, looks up values, and marks the option you choose. It does not solve.

Saathi is a digital exam scribe for visually impaired students. It reads the question paper aloud, looks up values when you ask, and marks the option you choose. It will not solve, hint, or tell you which answer is correct.

What it does - and does not

The scribe reads. You think. You answer.

Reads the paper

It reads competitive exam papers aloud in English and Hindi, including directions, statements, conclusions, options, tables, and arrangements.

Looks up values

It looks up values when you ask.

Marks your option

It marks the option you choose. Review is only your answer sheet - Saathi will not check if these are correct.

It does not solve

It will not solve, hint, or tell you which answer is correct. The thinking stays with you.

Using Saathi

Say the paper name, or select it. Saathi will read from the first question. You can speak or type commands to repeat, hear options, go next or previous, skip, flag, and mark an answer. When you finish, you can preview your marked answers. Saathi will not check if these are correct. This is only your answer sheet.
